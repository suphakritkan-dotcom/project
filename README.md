# Footballer Ranker
#### Video Demo:  <URL HERE>
#### Description:
Footballer Ranker is a simple, interactive web game that lets you rank five randomly picked football players from 1 (best) to 5 (worst). Instead of seeing all five players at once and easily putting them in order, the game shows you one player at a time. You look at the player on the screen including their jersey number, position, country, clubs, era, and a short highlight or stat, and then you choose which open spot from #1 to #5 you want to put them in. Because you do not know who will show up next, you have to think carefully. If you put an okay player in #1 early on, a superstar might appear later and you will have nowhere to put them at the top. Once you place all five players into spots #1 through #5, the round ends and shows a final summary of your picks, lets you copy your ranking to paste somewhere else, and lets you start a brand-new round with fresh players by clicking the green **New Round** button in the top-right corner of the screen.

I built this project as my CS50 Final Project because ranking football players is a fun topic that leads to lots of debates and opinions among friends. Everyone has different views on players from different eras, positions, and styles of play, so turning a simple 1-to-5 ranking list into a step-by-step game makes it much more engaging than a static list. You can play with a mix of everyone, or you can switch between **All Players**, **Legends**, and **Modern** to focus on classic icons or current stars. Whether you are playing on a laptop, desktop monitor, or phone screen, the page adjusts so the player card and ranking slots stay easy to read and tap.

### What Each File Does

This project is split into three main files inside the project folder so that each part has a clear job. Those files are `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js`. Keeping them separate makes the project much easier to read, organize, and update compared to putting every line of code into a single file.

**`index.html`** is the skeleton or structure of the webpage. It tells the web browser what parts exist on the page and in what order they appear. Inside `index.html`, there is the **New Round** button at the top right, a header with the title **Footballer Ranker**, a short explanation of the rules, and three pool buttons (**All Players**, **Legends**, and **Modern**). Below the header is the main game area, which has two parts. The first part is a card area that displays the current player's details. The second part is a slots area where your five ranking choices (`#1` through `#5`) are shown. At the bottom, there is a summary box that stays hidden while you are still playing and becomes visible after you rank all five players. At the very bottom of the body, `index.html` links `styles.css` for appearance and loads `script.js` so the page can respond to clicks and update text dynamically.

**`styles.css`** controls how the webpage looks and how it fits on different screen sizes. In `styles.css`, I set up color variables at the top so the dark background, card panels, borders, text colors, and green button accent stay consistent across the whole page. It uses flexbox and CSS Grid to arrange the top bar, group the pool buttons, place the player card next to the five ranking slots on wider screens, and add spacing between elements so nothing feels cramped. It also adds hover effects on buttons and ranking slots so you can clearly see which spot you are about to click. Finally, using a media query for smaller screens (`max-width: 720px`), `styles.css` stacks the player card and ranking slots into a single column and adjusts top spacing so the game works smoothly on mobile phones without overlapping the fixed **New Round** button.

**`script.js`** is the brain of the game and handles all the interactive actions behind the scenes. First, it stores a list of twenty well-known footballers with ten from the modern era and ten legends, along with details like their jersey number, position, nationality, clubs, era tag, and a short career highlight or stat. When a round starts, `script.js` filters the player list based on which pool button is active, shuffles the players randomly so the order is unpredictable each time, and picks five players for the round. It keeps track of which player you are currently viewing and which of the five ranking slots are still empty. When you click a ranking slot, `script.js` puts the current player into that slot, locks that slot so it cannot be overwritten during the same round, and moves to the next player. Once all five slots are filled, `script.js` reveals the final ranking summary, highlights your `#1` and `#5` choices, and enables the **Copy Ranking** button so you can copy your list to your clipboard. Clicking **New Round** or switching pools resets the round cleanly with a new set of five players.
